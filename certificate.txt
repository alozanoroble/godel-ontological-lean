How to read this
----------------

Neither Godel's nor Scott's axioms appear in this list.  That is the point:
they are not axioms of the Lean development, they are *hypotheses*, and every
theorem is a conditional --- which is what claims of this kind should be.  Had
they been declared with Lean's `axiom` command, the development would have been
globally inconsistent and this certificate worthless.

What does appear is only Lean's own three foundational axioms:

  propext           propositional extensionality
  Classical.choice  the axiom of choice
  Quot.sound        soundness of quotient types

and these enter at exactly two places, both classical "suppose not" steps:
`possibly_exists` (Theorem 1) and `positive_of_god`.

The five axiom-free results are all explicit models, checked constructively.
In particular the consistency of Scott's system is certified by an inspectable
model, not by a model finder.

The refutation of Godel's axioms takes NO frame hypothesis at all, so it is not
an artefact of choosing S5: the axioms are inconsistent in K, the weakest
normal modal logic.  The positive argument takes only symmetry --- the logic B
--- not the reflexivity and transitivity of S5.

Both statements are checked, not asserted: frame conditions are hypotheses of
the Lean theorems rather than properties baked into the semantics, so their
absence from a statement is a fact about the proof.  Note also that
`serial_of_scott` and `refl_of_scott` DERIVE seriality and reflexivity from
Scott's axioms rather than assuming them.

Other hypotheses: `Nonempty W` is needed for the refutation (with no worlds
every axiom is vacuous).  Nonemptiness of the individual type `I` is NOT needed
anywhere --- individuals are produced, never postulated.


The two headline pairs
----------------------

1. What Godel's missing conjunct costs:

     toy_not_A5           Godel's  A5, P(NE),  FAILS in the one-world model
     toy_satisfies_scott  Scott's  A5, P(NES), HOLDS in the same model

   Same structure, same notion of positivity, opposite verdicts.  The only
   difference between NE and NES is the conjunct phi(x) in the definition of
   essence.

2. What modal collapse costs:

     accessibility_eq     Scott's axioms + symmetry force  r w v <-> v = w
     iso_satisfies_scott  Scott's axioms hold over a world set of ANY size

   Together: models exist at every cardinality, but every one of them is a
   disjoint heap of isolated one-world models.  No world can see another, so
   box and diamond are the identity operation (box_iff, dia_iff) and the modal
   language in which the argument is stated has no content left.  The answer to
   "is there a non-degenerate model?" is yes in cardinality, no in modal
   structure.


Provenance
----------

The inconsistency, the Empty Essence Lemma and the K-refutation are due to
Benzmuller and Woltzenlogel Paleo, "The inconsistency in Godel's ontological
argument: a success story for AI in metaphysics", IJCAI-16, pp. 936-942
(section 4.1); they reconstructed it in Isabelle/HOL.  Theorems 1-3 are
Godel's; modal collapse is Sobel's; the consistency of Scott's system was
established in the same IJCAI paper with a model finder.  The frame reading of
modal collapse is standard --- the condition  forall x y, r x y -> y = x  is
stated directly as a rendering of MC in the literature on computational
variants of the argument.

None of this is new mathematics.  What this development contributes is an
independent Lean 4 mechanization with the dependency structure made explicit:
the frame-condition accounting above, and the fact that every result is a
conditional resting on nothing but Lean's own three axioms.

An explicit two-world model.  W = Bool; world `false` sees both worlds, world
`true` sees only itself (r w v  iff  w = true -> v = true).  One individual.
A property is positive, at EITHER world, exactly when the individual has it
at `true`.

The frame is reflexive (nsR_refl) and transitive (nsR_trans) but not symmetric
(nsR_not_symm) -- an S4 frame.

In it:
  ns_satisfies_scott        all six of Scott's axioms HOLD
  ns_not_T3                 Theorem 3 FAILS at `false`
  ns_not_modal_collapse     modal collapse FAILS
  ns_not_accessibility_eq   the frame is not equality

So symmetry cannot be weakened in the S4 direction, and more importantly:

  *** Scott's axioms do not entail modal collapse. ***

They entail it in B and S5 -- which is where the argument is always presented,
so the standard objection stands where it is usually made.  But collapse is a
consequence of the axioms TOGETHER WITH a symmetric frame, not of the axioms
alone.  Symmetry does exactly one job (exists_god): carrying box-exists-God
from a merely possible world back to the actual one, the B schema
diamond-box-p -> p.  That is also the step Anderson's and Fitting's variants
are reported to restructure away, which is why they are valid in K and T and
avoid the collapse.

All six results are constructive and depend on no axioms at all.

Fitting needs no frame condition because his God-likeness, evaluated at w, is
fixed by the positivity facts AT w; a witness found at an accessible world is
a witness simpliciter, so nothing has to be carried back.  That carrying-back
is exactly what symmetry bought in Scott's `exists_god` (see the addendum
above), so this is the same fact seen from the other side.

Anderson pays MORE in frame strength than Scott does (full S5, not just B),
and still avoids the collapse -- so frame strength is not by itself what
brings the collapse; the shape of the essence clause is.


Both avoid modal collapse
-------------------------

  Fitting.noCollapse_axioms / Fitting.noCollapse

and axiom-free.

Mechanisms, which differ:

  Fitting   the essence clause quantifies over EXTENSIONS (world-independent
            sets).  The collapse proof for Scott smuggled the sentence p in
            as the constant INTENSIONAL property  \y w. p w , which tracks p
            from world to world.  No extension can do that, so the
            substitution has no counterpart.

  Anderson  God-likeness and essence are stated entirely in terms of box, so
            a merely contingent sentence never satisfies the antecedent of
            the essence clause.


An observation from the certificate
-----------------------------------

Anderson's monotheism, god_transfers, god_essential and box_exists_of_god are
all CONSTRUCTIVE -- no axioms at all.  In Scott's system the corresponding
step (positive_of_god) is classical, because it argues by contradiction
through A1b.  Anderson has no A1b, and the classicality goes with it.  Only
Theorem 1, and hence Anderson.T3, remains classical.  Fitting keeps A1b and
keeps the classical step.


Simplifications (stated in the paper, section 9.4)
--------------------------------------------------

Fitting's formalization here carries an explicit existence predicate and uses
actualist quantifiers.  That is NOT optional: a Fitting extension is
world-independent, so with constant domains "S is empty" would be
world-independent, Theorem 1 would give actual rather than merely possible
exemplification, and T3 would follow WITHOUT EVER USING A5 -- which is not
Fitting's argument.

Anderson's formalization uses constant domains, where that costs nothing
structural: his God-likeness is intensional, so exists-x-God(x) still varies
from world to world and A5 still does real work.

NOT modelled: Fitting's intension/extension operators, hence the de re / de
dicto distinction.  Fitting.T3 is the de re reading (God-likeness frozen at
the world of evaluation); Benzmuller and Fuenmayor report the de dicto
reading is invalid.

Following Benzmuller and Fuenmayor, arXiv:1910.08955, and the AFP entry
"Types, Tableaus and Godel's God in Isabelle/HOL" (2017), which render
Anderson's variant as Fitting presents it.

A correction to the previous addendum
-------------------------------------

The earlier note said the de re / de dicto distinction "needs Fitting's
intension/extension operators" and was therefore not modelled.  That was
wrong twice over.

First, the distinction needs no extra machinery in a shallow embedding.
Fitting's `down' operator is needed to write his SYNTAX; semantically the
difference is only where a world variable is bound:

  de re     forall v, r w v -> exists z, Ex z v  /\  God P z w
  de dicto  forall v, r w v -> exists z, Ex z v  /\  God P z v

Both were already expressible in the file as written.

Second, the failure Benzmuller and Fuenmayor report is NOT a failure of the
de dicto reading of the CONCLUSION.  Their T3 is the POSSIBILITY statement,
refuted in their Part I, where only A1a, A1b, A2 and T2 are available.  Their
Part II adds A4, and in section 5.4 their GodNecExists-v1 -- the de dicto
NECESSARY existence -- is PROVED.  So with the full axiom set both readings
of the conclusion hold.


What is now settled
-------------------

  Fitting.T3           de re  necessary existence   -- theorem, logic K
  Fitting.T3_deDicto   de dicto necessary existence -- theorem, logic K

The bridge is `god_stable': A4 carries the extension godExt P w forward along
r, so anything God-like at v lies in it and is therefore God-like at w; and
God-like individuals are unique at a world (Fitting.monotheism, the same A1b
trick as Anderson's).  So the two readings agree.

  Fitting.dd_axioms                 Part I axioms HOLD in a 2-world model
  Fitting.dd_possible_deRe          de re possibility HOLDS there
  Fitting.dd_not_possible_deDicto   de dicto possibility FAILS there
  Fitting.dd_not_A4                 A4 fails there, as it must

The model: two worlds and two individuals; each world sees only the OTHER
world (r w v iff w /= v); the individual existing at a world is the OTHER one
(Ex z v iff z /= v); a set is positive at w iff it contains w's own
individual.  God-likeness at w is then "being w's own individual" -- which is
exactly the individual that does not exist at w.

Two structural facts fall out of the construction:

  - The separation needs a NON-UNIVERSAL frame.  A4 propagates positivity
    along r, so over the universal relation it makes positivity, and hence
    God-likeness, world-independent, and the two readings identical.

  - The separation needs VARYING DOMAINS.  The room for the readings to come
    apart is an individual God-like as read off at w that does not EXIST at
    v.  This is a second reason, independent of the one in the previous
    addendum, why the existence predicate in Fitting.lean is not optional.

All four countermodel results are constructive and axiom-free.

The system
----------

Hajek merges A1 and A2 into one weaker axiom:

  H:A12   ( P(phi) /\ box forall x (phi x -> psi x) )  ->  not P(not psi)

Anderson's A1a is the case psi := phi; A2 is NOT assumed (the conclusion is
only that not-psi fails to be positive, not that psi is positive).  He then
rebuilds God-likeness on the derived notion

  P#(phi)  :=  exists psi ( P(psi) /\ box forall y (psi y -> phi y) )
  G^H(x)   :=  forall phi ( box phi(x)  <->  P#(phi) )

keeping Anderson's essence and necessary existence unchanged.


*** A4 and A5 are superfluous ***
---------------------------------

This is checked by the type, not asserted:

  theorem T3 (hs : Symm r) (h : AxCore r P) (w : W) : ...

`AxCore` contains ONLY H:A12 and A3.  A4 (positivity is necessary) and A5
(necessary existence is positive) live in the separate structure `Ax`, and
appear nowhere in the derivation.  Benzmuller, Weber and Woltzenlogel Paleo
report exactly this superfluousness, and add that A4 and A5 are nonetheless
INDEPENDENT of the rest -- superfluous without being redundant.  The
independence needs a further pair of models and is NOT formalized here.

The mechanism is `box_exists_of_god', and it is short.  G^H is positive by A3,
so it is trivially necessarily implied by a positive property -- itself -- and
therefore P#(G^H) holds outright.  The biconditional in G^H(g) converts that
directly into box G^H(g): the God-like individual is necessarily God-like.
Where Scott needs A5 and an essence argument to make God-like existence
necessary, Hajek's definition delivers it with no axioms beyond A3 and NO
FRAME CONDITION AT ALL.

Frame condition for T3: symmetry only, used once, at the same place as in
Scott's system -- carrying the conclusion back from a merely possible world.

Modal collapse
--------------

`Hajek.noCollapse_axioms' is a two-world model over the UNIVERSAL relation --
an S5 frame -- satisfying ALL of AOE including A4 and A5, so it also certifies
the consistency of the full axiom set.  `Hajek.noCollapse' shows collapse
fails there.  Both constructive and axiom-free.

Mechanism: as with Anderson's variant, G^H is stated entirely in terms of box,
so a merely contingent sentence never reaches the biconditional.

Note `A1a_of_A12' and `box_exists_of_god' are constructive.  T1 is classical
and additionally needs propositional extensionality, for `pneg_pneg' -- the
derivation instantiates H:A12 at psi := not-phi and must identify not-not-phi
with phi.

Following Benzmuller, Weber and Woltzenlogel Paleo, "Computer-Assisted
Analysis of the Anderson-Hajek Ontological Controversy", Logica Universalis 11
(2017), pp. 139-151, Figure 3.  Hajek's second and third emendations (AOE0 and
AOE*, their Figures 4 and 5) are not formalized here.
