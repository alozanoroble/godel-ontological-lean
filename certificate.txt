Note: Anderson's Theorem 3 in KB, and the redundancy of A4 and A5
-----------------------------------------------------------------

The 2026-09-19 certificate derived Anderson's T3 from reflexivity,
transitivity AND symmetry, using all five of his axioms, and recorded the gap
to the published KB result as open work.  That gap is now closed.

  'Godel.Anderson.box_god'       does not depend on any axioms
  'Godel.Anderson.god_back'      does not depend on any axioms
  'Godel.Anderson.exists_god'    [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]
  'Godel.Anderson.T3'            [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]

T3 now takes `AxCore` -- A1a, A2, A3* -- and `Symm r`.  It does NOT take A4
or A5, and this is enforced by the type: `AxCore` has no such fields, so the
proof could not use them even by accident.

The argument.  Anderson's God-likeness is a BICONDITIONAL,

    G(x)  :=  forall phi ( P(phi) <-> box phi(x) ).

Instantiate it at phi := G.  Axiom A3* says P(G), so the biconditional yields
box G(x) directly: a God-like individual is NECESSARILY God-like.  That is
`box_god`, and it needs no frame condition and no other axiom.  Everything
else follows:

  - T1 (A1a, A2) gives a God-like g at SOME world v accessible from w;
  - symmetry gives r v w, and box_god at v carries g back to w  (god_back);
  - box_god at w pushes g out to every world accessible from w  (T3).

A1a and A2 are used only inside T1, and only to guarantee that w has a
successor at all.

Monotheism improved likewise: it previously assumed reflexivity, to bring
`box (y = x)` down to `y = x`.  T1 already supplies an accessible world, so
`Godel.Anderson.monotheism` now needs NO frame condition.

The two redundancy results, previously cited from Benzmuller-Weber-
Woltzenlogel Paleo (2017) and now proved:

  'Godel.Anderson.ne_universal'    does not depend on any axioms
  'Godel.Anderson.A5_of_A2_A3p'    does not depend on any axioms
  'Godel.Anderson.A4_of_core'      [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]

A5 in K.  Anderson's essence is also a biconditional,

    phi ess x  :=  forall psi ( box psi(x) <-> box forall y (phi(y) -> psi(y)) ).

Put psi := phi.  The right-hand side becomes box forall y (phi(y) -> phi(y)),
a validity, so box phi(x) holds.  Hence anything that is an essence of x is
necessarily had by x, so necessarily instantiated -- by x.  So NE holds of
EVERY individual at EVERY world (`ne_universal`), with no axioms and no frame
condition whatsoever.  A2 applied to A3* then lifts that to P(NE), which is
A5.  Anderson's necessary existence is the universally true property, which
is a sharper statement than redundancy: A5 is not merely derivable, it is
contentless.

A4 in K4B.  Given P(phi) at w and r w u: exists_god gives a God-like g at w
(symmetry), box_god makes it God-like at u too, so P(phi) at u reduces to
box phi(g) at u.  God-likeness of g at w turns the hypothesis into box phi(g)
at w, and transitivity carries those witnesses to every world accessible
from u.

Both match the frame conditions reported in the 2017 study -- A5 already in
K, A4 in K4B.  NOTHING HERE IS NEW MATHEMATICS.  The published results were
obtained by automated theorem provers; what is new in this development is
only that the frame condition and the axiom list are now carried by Lean's
type checker rather than asserted in a comment.

Scope.  All of this is for the CONSTANT-DOMAIN (possibilist) reading used
throughout this development.  The 2017 study finds A4 still redundant but A5
INDEPENDENT under the mixed reading Anderson floats, with a countermodel to
T3 itself.  None of the above transfers to that reading.

Anderson's own essence route is retained for comparison:

  'Godel.Anderson.T3_viaEssence'  [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]

Same conclusion, but consuming reflexivity, transitivity, A4 and A5.  Keeping
both side by side is the point: the S5 was a property of that argument, never
of Anderson's system.

Hajek is unaffected.  His A5 is stated with Anderson's NE, so `ne_universal`
applies to it -- but Hajek does not assume A2, so `A5_of_A2_A3p` does not run
in his system, and the "superfluous but independent" verdict for AOE stands.
