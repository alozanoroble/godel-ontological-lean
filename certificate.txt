Note 1: Fitting's de re Theorem 3 needs neither A1b nor A4
-----------------------------------------------------------

  'Godel.Fitting.singleton_ess'  does not depend on any axioms
  'Godel.Fitting.T3'             [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]

Fitting.T3 previously took the full record `Ax`.  Tracing it showed A4 was
never used; the reduction below removes A1b as well.  T3 now takes `AxT3`,
which is A1a + A2 + T2 + A5 and nothing else.  No frame condition (logic K).

A1b was entering through god_essential -> positive_of_god.  It is avoidable
because in Fitting's system essences are EXTENSIONS -- plain sets -- and the
singleton {g} is an essence of g outright:

  Ess S x w  :=  S x  AND  forall T, T x -> Entails S T w
  Entails S T w  :=  forall v, r w v -> forall z, Ex z v -> S z -> T z

With S := {g}, the first conjunct is g = g.  For the second, any z with
S z at an accessible world IS g, so the property T being asked for is one
g already has by hypothesis.  No positivity, hence no A1b.

A5 then says g lies in the necessary-existence extension, and NE applied to
{g} says g itself exists at every accessible world -- which is exactly the
de re conclusion.  god_essential is never consulted.

This is a clean result: singletons are unimpeachably extensions in Fitting's
system, so nothing here turns on a contested comprehension principle.

The de dicto reading is unaffected and still needs both:

  'Godel.Fitting.god_stable'   uses A4 (and A1b, via monotheism)
  'Godel.Fitting.T3_deDicto'   takes the full `Ax`

which gives the clean division the structure now makes visible:

  A5 drives necessary EXISTENCE, de re.
  A4 drives WORLD-STABILITY of God-likeness.
  A1b enters only through monotheism, which stability needs to identify
      the witness at the accessible world with the one at home.
  Together: necessary existence de dicto.


Note 2: Scott's Theorem 3 without A1b or A4 -- READ THE CAVEAT
---------------------------------------------------------------

  'Godel.hae_scottEss'   does not depend on any axioms
  'Godel.blind_of_god'   does not depend on any axioms
  'Godel.scott_T3'       [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]

scott_T3 now takes ScottCore (A1a, A2, A3), A5, and symmetry.  Scott's own
route is preserved as scott_T3_viaT2, which additionally uses A1b and A4.

The mechanism.  Fix g and v and take the property "being g, at v":

  phi  :=  lambda z u. (z = g AND u = v)

This is a Scott-essence of g at v with NO axioms (hae_scottEss).  Scott's
essence clause asks that phi necessarily entail every property g has at v;
but the only way to bear phi at an accessible world u is to be g with u = v,
at which point the entailed property is being asked for at v itself, where it
holds by hypothesis.

Now let g be God-like at v.  D1 plus A5 give g Scott-necessary existence, and
applying that to phi says every world accessible from v IS v (blind_of_god).
So A5 and D1 alone isolate the world of a God-like individual.  T1 applied to
A3 puts a God-like individual at some world accessible from w; symmetry makes
w one of the worlds that world sees; so they are the same world, and
everything follows.

*** CAVEAT.  This may be a fact about the encoding rather than about Scott's
*** system.  Whether a property may mention a particular world is a question
*** about the comprehension principle of the higher-order language, not about
*** Scott's axioms.  The shallow embedding used here makes every function
*** I -> W -> Prop a property, so the haecceity above is available.  The
*** published dependency analyses reach Theorem 3 through Theorem 2, and
*** hence through A4; the divergence most likely lives in exactly this
*** lemma.  NO CLAIM OF NOVELTY IS MADE, and this should be checked against
*** the literature before being relied on.

Contrast Fitting, where the same idea needs no caveat: his essences are sets,
so the bare singleton {g} suffices and no world is mentioned.  That the trick
is clean there and questionable here is itself the interesting part -- it is
the intensional/extensional distinction showing up as a difference in what
the essence clause lets you get away with.


Note 3: modal collapse, audited at two levels
----------------------------------------------

These are different questions and the answers differ.

  'Godel.modal_collapse'         ScottCore + A5 + symmetry
  'Godel.modal_collapse_of_T3'   A1b + A4 + seriality + symmetry + T3

The first expands everything to primitives and inherits the short route of
Note 2, so it uses neither A1b nor A4.

The second takes necessary God-like existence as a HYPOTHESIS and asks what
else is needed.  Answer: A1b and A4 (through Theorem 2), seriality, and
symmetry -- and none of A1a, A2, A3, A5, which appear only inside T3's own
proof.  This is the route the published summaries describe (collapse from
A1, A4, B, D1, T3).

Stating both prevents "what the lemma as coded consumes" from being confused
with "what the theory needs from primitives".

    The S4 countermodel is NOT symmetric, so nothing above applies to it and
    T3 and collapse still fail there.  Symmetry remains necessary.
  iso_satisfies_scott, toy_satisfies_scott
    Frames where r is equality; blind_of_god holds trivially.

Status vocabulary used from here on, since these are genuinely different:

  USED BY THIS PROOF   -- what the signature consumes
  DERIVABLE            -- provable from the rest (Anderson's A4, A5)
  INDEPENDENT          -- a countermodel exists without it

Nothing below "DERIVABLE" has been established for Scott or Fitting; the
reductions above are of the first kind, and no independence result is
claimed for any axiom of either system.


Addendum (same day, after referee review of this certificate)
--------------------------------------------------------------

Two corrections to the notes above.

1. The diagnosis in Note 2 was wrong in its emphasis.  It said the divergence
   from the published analyses "most likely" lives in hae_scottEss and that
   the reduction is probably an encoding artefact.  That prejudges a question
   that has two legitimate answers, and the right statement is neutral:

     The reduction depends on the property domain containing world-indexed
     haecceities H(g,v) := lambda z u. (z = g AND u = v).  The shallow Lean
     embedding contains all such functions.  Whether that is faithful to
     Scott's intended property quantification depends on the semantics
     adopted.  Under FULL INTENSIONAL SEMANTICS, where the property domain is
     all of I x W -> Prop, the shortcut is a genuine consequence of Scott's
     definitions plus A5.  Under a RESTRICTED or HENKIN property domain, or
     one given by a modal comprehension schema over the object language, the
     question is whether H(g,v) is in the domain at all -- and a standard
     modal object language has no term naming the current world, so
     definability is not automatic.

   Some published presentations of Scott do state the background theory with a
   comprehension schema rather than identifying properties with arbitrary sets
   of individual-world pairs, which is what makes the distinction bite.

   One concrete data point, obtained by inspecting the published sources
   rather than by running anything.  The Isabelle/HOL embedding used by
   Benzmuller and collaborators (AFP, "Types, Tableaus and Godel's God",
   theory IHOML) declares

     typedecl i                     -- possible worlds
     typedecl e                     -- individuals
     type_synonym io  = (i => bool)
     type_synonym ise = (e => io)   -- properties

   so a property there is e => i => bool, the same function space as ours, and
   quantification over it is plain HOL quantification with no comprehension
   schema restricting property formation.  H(g,v) is therefore expressible and
   in range in their encoding too, and the reduction is unlikely to be
   peculiar to Lean.

   THIS IS NOT THE EXPERIMENT.  Nobody has asked their development for
   blind_of_god.  Two outcomes are worth distinguishing: if Isabelle proves it
   in their existing encoding, this is a previously unremarked consequence of
   the standard shallow HOL encoding; if their encoding cannot state or prove
   it, comparing the property types pinpoints where this Lean representation
   is stronger.  That experiment is the open question, and it is recorded as
   open, not settled.

2. "Symmetry is necessary" was an overclaim, here and elsewhere in this
   development.  The S4 countermodel (ns_*) shows that the symmetry hypothesis
   cannot simply be deleted from the KB results in favour of reflexivity and
   transitivity.  It does NOT show symmetry is necessary in the absolute
   sense: some frame condition weaker than, or incomparable with, symmetry
   might still suffice, and no correspondence or minimality theorem here
   excludes that.  The wording has been corrected in Sanity.lean, the paper
   and the handoff.  certificate-20260919.txt carries the old phrasing and is
   left as it stands, being a dated record; read this addendum against it.

Neither correction changes a single Lean proof.  The dumps above stand.
